Care at a glance

Bleeding

  • Bite gently on gauze for 30-45 minutes, then replace if needed.
  • Some oozing is normal for the first day.
  • Avoid spitting, rinsing hard and using straws.

Swelling and comfort

  • Use a cold pack on the cheek, 20 minutes on and 20 off, the first day.
  • Take medicine only as directed by Dr. Hong.
  • Keep your head raised when resting.

Eating

  • Soft, cool foods the first day.
  • Avoid hot, crunchy or spicy foods until comfortable.
  • Chew on the other side.

Cleaning

  • Brush the other teeth as usual; avoid the area at first.
  • Gentle saltwater rinses starting the day after.
  • No smoking or vaping; it slows healing.
