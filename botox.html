Botox for jaw clenching and headaches

Botox relaxes the muscles it is placed in. In dentistry it is used for muscles that work too hard, which can cause jaw pain, worn teeth and headaches. Dr. Hong can see you at your location.

What it can help with

Jaw clenching and teeth grinding (bruxism)

  • Can ease jaw pain, tight jaw muscles and morning headaches.
  • May reduce wear on teeth, crowns and fillings.
  • [Dr. Hong: confirm wording. Use for bruxism is typically off-label.]

Headaches and migraine

  • Botox is FDA approved for chronic migraine (headaches on 15 or more days a month).
  • Treatment is placed in set areas of the head, neck and shoulders, repeated about every 3 months.
  • [Dr. Hong: confirm what you will treat and when you refer to a neurologist or the patient's doctor.]

Botox is not right for everyone. Tell Dr. Hong if you are pregnant or nursing, have a nerve or muscle condition, or have had a reaction to Botox.

Care after Botox

First few hours

  • Stay upright for about 4 hours; do not lie flat.
  • Do not press, rub or massage the treated areas.

First day

  • Skip hard exercise, saunas and hot tubs.
  • Small bumps, redness or a little bruising can happen and fade quickly.

What to expect

  • Results start in about 3 to 7 days and build over about 2 weeks.
  • Effects wear off slowly over about 3 months.

Call us

  • Trouble swallowing, speaking or a drooping eyelid or smile that bothers you.
  • Hives, swelling or rash.
  • Trouble breathing: call 911.
